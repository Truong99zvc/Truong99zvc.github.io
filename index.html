Fruit Detection with Faster R-CNN
Feb 2025 – Jun 2025 · CS231.P22 at UIT

Engineered a two-stage object detection pipeline for 11 fruit categories. Automated data preprocessing by converting YOLO annotations to COCO format, then evaluated three distinct training strategies to find the optimal approach.

★ Outcome
Peak mAP@[.5:.95] of 0.7243 using full model fine-tuning, generalizing to diverse real-world environments.
PyTorch Faster R-CNN ResNet50-FPN COCO Format CUDA
02 — Computer Vision
BiM-RNet: Video Frame Interpolation
Sep – Dec 2025 · CS529.Q11 at UIT

Reproduced the BiM-VFI architecture on Vimeo-90K, then proposed BiM-RNet with a ResNet-based Encoder for enhanced feature extraction in complex motion scenarios. Deployed as an interactive web interface.

★ Outcome
